## 3. Brand Asset Usage Policy & Nominative Fair Use

### 3.1 Purpose of Visual Indicators
Icons, logos, and badges representing payment methods or cryptocurrency networks are included in the Nyayota user interface to:
- Clearly differentiate supported network rails (e.g. Bitcoin Mainnet vs. Ethereum Mainnet vs. BNB Smart Chain).
- Assist users in preventing irreversible token-loss errors by matching their wallet network to the exact protocol indicated.
- Distinguish between operational donation channels (Cryptocurrency) and non-operational planned roadmaps (Binance Pay, Bank Transfer, Other Methods).

### 3.2 Display Standards
- Logos are rendered faithfully according to each organization's public branding specifications, maintaining proper aspect ratios, clear space, and official color hex values.
- Brand marks are never altered, caricature-stylized, or combined with other trademarks.
- Where an integration is non-operational, it is explicitly accompanied by a lock icon, a "Coming soon" or "Under consideration" badge, and plain-text disclosures confirming that no payment can be processed through that method.
